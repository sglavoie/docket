Welcome
	Move, fold and zoom:
		Press j and k to move between these rows; gg returns to the first and G reaches the last.
		Press H to reach this heading, h to fold it, then l to unfold it.
		Press Enter on this row to zoom into it; Esc brings you back.
		Press ] for the next heading and [ to come back; then ] to continue.
	Add and type:
		Press o here, type a task of your own, then Esc to keep it; u takes it back.
		Press A on this row, add a word, then Esc; I starts at the beginning and cc retypes the text.
		Press w to step into this row; w and b move by word, cw changes a word, then Esc twice returns.
		Press o and type two words; Left then Enter splits the line, and Backspace at its start joins the pieces with a space.
	States and sigils:
		Press x to close this task and x again to reopen it; the heading counts your progress.
		Press \! to flag this row, then t to pick it for Today; press each again to clear it.
		Press A here and append \#practice and \@fri then Esc to keep the tag and deadline as fields.
		Press # then practice and Enter to stamp this row; -#practice and Enter removes the tag.
		Press $ then waiting and Enter here; u restores its state. Try \$doing or \$parked too.
		Press @ then \@mon and Enter for a start day; * then 2w and Enter repeats this row. Use u twice to restore it.
		Press # then est=30m and Enter here; see the time added to the header, then u to remove it.
	Counts and operators:
		Press 2j to move two shown rows; 2k returns. :numbers and Enter shows each row's distance.
		Press yy here, then p to put a new copy below; u removes the copy.
		Press dd on this row, then u to restore it; d followed by j deletes this row and the next in one step.
		Press >> on this row to indent it under the row above, then << to bring it back; J and K reorder it.
		Press \! here, j then . to flag the next row too; u twice restores both.
		This task receives the repeated flag from the row above.
	Selection:
		Press v here to pick this row and step down; press v again to pick the next.
		Press \! to flag both selected rows in one step; u takes it back and Esc clears the selection.
		Press V then j to sweep two rows, x to close both, u to undo, then Esc to clear.
		This task closes together with the row above in the sweep.
	Filter:
		Press / then practice and Enter to keep a text filter; Esc shows the outline again. #practice
		Type /#practice then Enter to find this tagged row; Tab switches flat and context views, or completes a sigil being typed. #practice
		Type /$next then Enter for this project's first workable task; Esc returns to all rows.
		Type /$first then Enter for the first workable sibling under every parent; Esc returns.
		Press / then "first workable" and Enter to match that phrase; Esc returns, then n and N walk its matches.
	Capture and travel:
		Press a, type a task, then Enter to capture it in the Inbox; Esc ends capture.
		Press a and type Try this %Welcome and press Enter then Esc to file a task in this project.
		Press f, type Inbox and Enter to visit it; Ctrl+O returns here and Ctrl+I goes forward again.
		Press m here, type Inbox and Enter to move this row there; u returns it to this heading.
		Press Tab to focus the sidebar, k to visit a place, then Enter to work there; Ctrl+O returns.
	Undo and commands:
		Press dd on this row, u to undo, Ctrl+R to redo, and u once more to keep it.
		Type :help and Enter for the reference; ] and [ walk its headings, and Esc returns here.
		Press Tab to focus the sidebar, where Welcome is chosen, and dd to remove this tutorial; u brings it back. :tutor brings a fresh one.
